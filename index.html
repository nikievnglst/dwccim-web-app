Saturday Agenda

9:00 – 9:30 AM Call Time
9:30 – 10:00 AM Devotion (15-min grace period)
10:00 AM – 12:00 PM Practice

No devotion, no practice. Attendance at devotion is required in order to join practice.

Members who arrive late to practice are welcome to observe and receive guidance from the team, but may not play during the session — this helps avoid disrupting the group's focus. Please handle this with maturity; if this policy is a source of frustration, it's best to step away for the day rather than create tension within the team.

Team Rules

  1. Attendance at the Saturday practice immediately before your scheduled Sunday is required.
  2. All members must attend Saturday practice, even if not scheduled to sing, dance, or play an instrument that week.
  3. Exceptions are made only for important prior commitments or genuine emergencies.
  4. If you are unable to attend the next Saturday practice, notify the team as soon as possible through the group chat — at least 1–2 weeks in advance when known ahead of time, or a minimum of 2 hours before practice in urgent situations.
  5. If an emergency prevents you from attending Sunday service after having attended Saturday's practice, no replacement will take over the parts that were rehearsed — the team will proceed as prepared.
  6. All members are expected to attentively listen to the sermon during service (except teachers and assistant teachers serving in Children's Ministry).
  7. Members must dress modestly and appropriately at all times.

💬 Can't make it to a Saturday practice? Use the Team tab to mark it and get a ready-to-send message for the group chat.
